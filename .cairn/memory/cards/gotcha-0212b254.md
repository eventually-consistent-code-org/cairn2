Wave dispatch does not produce a claim comment, and the measured-duration design depends on one.

In phase 24, exactly ONE of seventeen issues carried a claim comment. The other sixteen were claimed with `issue_update(state: "in_progress")` and nothing else, because when the coordinator is dispatching a wave it is not narrating each issue — the worker gets a brief, not a tracker comment. Work.md step 4 asks for the comment; wave dispatch quietly skips it.

Why this matters more than the missing narration: issue #232 (phase 24.7, NOT YET BUILT) makes the claim-comment timestamp the FIRST rung of its duration ladder. Measured against the tracker today, sixteen of seventeen phase-24 issues return a claim-to-close delta of zero or garbage, because the earliest comment on them is the close evidence the close itself posted. One issue returned 5373 minutes, because its earliest comment predated the phase entirely.

So when 24.7 is built: rung (a), the claim comment, will carry INLINE work only. Rung (b), the in-progress transition the server observed itself, is the rung that actually carries wave work — which is most work. Build and test (b) as the primary path, not the fallback, and make sure a missing rung (a) records `wall_source` honestly rather than looking like "no data".

The cheaper alternative worth weighing at plan time: have the coordinator post the claim comment even when dispatching, which restores rung (a) and the paper trail together. That is a verb-prose fix, not a server one.
